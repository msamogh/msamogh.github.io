I did my Ph.D. in Computer Science at the University of Florida with Kristy Boyer and Bonnie Dorr, on Neurosymbolic Authoring and Test-Time Compute Allocation for Generative Educational Simulations.

The thread through my work is simulating people with language models, and measuring when that works: generative agent-based models checked against dozens of behavioral targets with verifiers that can't be gamed by ordering or verbosity, role-play fidelity, and synthetic users who talk the way real ones do. Before the Ph.D. I built conversational AI and ML systems at Rasa, Fractal Analytics and IBM.

Open to research roles in behavioral simulation, synthetic populations and LLM evaluation. msamogh@gmail.com
